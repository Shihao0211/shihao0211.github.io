ABOUT

Research at the interface of vascular biology and neural injury.

I am a physician-scientist investigating neurovascular remodeling, vascular smooth muscle cell plasticity, axonal degeneration, and white matter injury. My work integrates patient-derived iPSC and organoid models, spatial and single-cell multi-omics, in vivo disease models, imaging, and translational biomarker studies.
