ABOUT

Physician-scientist focused on mechanisms and translation.

My work lies at the intersection of neurotrauma, axon biology, and cerebrovascular disease. I am particularly interested in how molecular pathways of axonal degeneration interact with vascular and glial responses after acute and chronic neurological injury.

My current research integrates in vivo disease models, quantitative neuropathology, imaging, molecular assays, and translational therapeutic development.
